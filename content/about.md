Hi! I'm a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Drexel University and a Research Assistant in the SOAR Lab. My research focuses on trustworthy AI for software engineering. I investigate how large language models and AI agents reason about software, and develop methods to improve their reliability, interpretability, and performance in automated program repair and code generation, and developer-facing AI tools. More broadly, I'm interested in building trustworthy AI systems through empirical evaluation and model analysis.
